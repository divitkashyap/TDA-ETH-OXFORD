Even though cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology are growing, there are still big challenges that stop them from being widely used:

- High Volatility: Cryptocurrency prices can swing wildly, making them hard to use for everyday transactions

- Regulatory Uncertainty: Different countries have different rules, making it difficult for blockchain projects to grow globally

- Limited Accessibility: Many people find crypto confusing or don’t have access to the tools needed to use it

- Liquidity and Stability Issues: Some projects struggle to maintain a stable market for their tokens, which can make them risky to use

These challenges make it hard for people to trust and adopt cryptocurrencies.
